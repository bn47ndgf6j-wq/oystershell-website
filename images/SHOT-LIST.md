# Oystershell website photo shot list

Drop photos into this folder with exactly these names. The site picks them up automatically; any missing photo shows its placeholder.

**Format:** JPG, landscape, 4:3, at least 2000 px wide, under 500 KB each (squash at squoosh.app). Photo 1 on each page is the large hero tile.
**Avoid:** other companies' logos or screens (Official Charts, Gamestec, etc.), customers' faces without permission, pub names on signs.
